# Forward 001: daily paper research

This policy is frozen before the first real forward capture. It does not promote baseline-001 or qualify bets. The fitted coefficients stay fixed; no retraining, final-test performance evaluation, confidence interval or return claim is enabled.

A manual capture fetches a fresh, bounded page chain of current-season games through tomorrow and one fresh three-book odds snapshot. Results checking fetches only game data. The current season is 2026–27, with regular-season boundaries and the Cup championship date sourced from the [NBA schedule announcement](https://pr.nba.com/2026-27-nba-regular-season-schedule). Provider date/pagination options and lifecycle classifications were checked against [BALLDONTLIE documentation](https://nba.balldontlie.io/); odds pairing follows [The Odds API's two-sided market schema](https://the-odds-api.com/liveapi/guides/v4/). Checked October 25, 2026.

The forecast cutoff is the actual creation time after retrieval. All inputs must have been observed by then, and the game must still be scheduled in the future. Only games on that Eastern calendar date are forecast. Exact home/away identity plus starts within five minutes joins the two providers. Explicit LA Clippers alias only; no fuzzy matching. Missing, ambiguous, started, postponed or unknown-status games stay visible as unavailable. Quote freshness limits (five minutes since retrieval, thirty minutes since book update) are operational exclusions, not evidence of predictive validity.

For each eligible game, reuse baseline-001's exact feature builder with its three-day score lag and fitted coefficients. Do not fit a new model. The historical seed is the audited dataset whose hash is locked in baseline-001; check config, model, source and snapshot integrity before forecasting. Reserved 2025 scores may update state for later forecasts; no 2025 model-performance metrics are opened. Current-season pages must be exhausted; unresolved earlier nonfinal games block affected teams instead of quietly assuming a result. An exhausted provider query is a bounded coverage observation, not proof that the provider omitted no games.

Display home/away win probabilities, expected home-minus-away margin, input-based explanation, and matching book prices. Market probability is each book's two moneyline implied probabilities normalized to sum to one. The displayed difference is diagnostic percentage points, not a qualified edge. A fair spread from expected margin is not a cover probability; spread prices are displayed without claiming EV or intervals. List games chronologically, not by an unvalidated ranking.

Save all captures, empty days, blocked-game reasons and failed operations in a private hash-linked ledger. Application writes append new records and verify the chain on reads; this is not external immutable storage or protection against an owner rewriting the whole chain. Original forecast inputs, model/config/policy hashes, data snapshot hashes, cutoff and prices are preserved. Never backdate a capture or label a manual run as a scheduled morning/pregame forecast.

For research evaluation, use the first successfully saved pre-tipoff forecast per game under this policy. Additional snapshots stay visible as updates but do not multiply sample size or replace a weak prediction. Append final-score observations and any later corrections; never alter the original capture. Check game identity, scheduled start, and final status. Rescheduling/identity conflicts require review. Evaluate all settled primary forecasts and compare model and mean same-cutoff within-book market probabilities on their common eligible subset. Report sample counts, probability errors and winner accuracy, with early samples explicitly inconclusive. No bet selection, stake, return, closing-line value, or actual-bet linkage is implemented here.

Captures are manual and opportunistic. This first record tests data flow and forecasting integrity; it does not establish the intended fixed-time morning/pregame policy. Freeze that separate policy and its evaluation requirements before making qualification claims.
