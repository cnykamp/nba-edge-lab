# Version history

## 0.5.0 — 2026-10-26

- Preregistered three fixed recency/context variants and two chronological fitting/calibration/evaluation splits before fitting, with no tuning repeats or final-test performance evaluation. Preserved original model/implementation hashes.
- Added prior-date observed-final forward state, offseason/recency weighting, back-to-backs, four/seven-day congestion and opponent-adjusted margins. Added contributing-game, season-mix and source-time audit displays.
- Added a converged penalized Newton fit, past-only Platt calibration, empirical margin prediction intervals, cover/push/loss probabilities, synthetic-line scoring and interval-coverage diagnostics. Logged both improvements and earlier-period calibration/coverage failures.
- Added a separate forward-003 candidate paper cohort, same-cutoff baseline shadow, preserved original cohorts and future review gates. No qualified best bet or shortlist, automatic retraining or promotion.
- Added validated append-only manual official-report observations, stale/incomplete/unresolved states and no invented injury-impact adjustment. No automated statistics scraping or paid services.
- Added causal, numerical, calibration-split, distribution, cohort/integrity and manual-review HTTP tests.

## 0.4.0 — 2026-10-25

- Added manual daily capture and score-check actions to the responsive dashboard, with CLI equivalents and bounded free-provider requests.
- Reused baseline-001's frozen features/weights for original future-game forecasts, expected margins and input-grounded explanations. No retraining, final-test performance evaluation or model promotion.
- Added same-event/book moneyline normalization, both sides' moneyline/spread prices, freshness/lead-time gates and explicit unavailable states. No cover probabilities, uncertainty intervals, qualified picks or ROI claims.
- Added a private hash-linked append-only capture/result ledger, correction successors and first-forecast accounting within separate policy cohorts.
- Preserved the first forward-001 capture when actual feeds differed by ten minutes. Registered forward-002 before another capture, allowing fifteen-minute event matching plus a fifteen-minute minimum forecast lead. Both records remain intact.
- Added local Origin/Host checks, serialized operations and tests for matching, timing, leakage, correction chains, accounting and integrity.

## 0.3.0 — Baseline research

- Added a resumable, rate-limited five-season historical backfill and independent regular-season audit.
- Recorded experiment splits, one-configuration budget, features, baselines and limitations before real-data fitting, with a separate source-backed eligibility correction for play-ins and Cup finals.
- Added chronological score-feature construction, regularized logistic and score-margin baselines, fixed expanding-window comparisons, calibration tables, and private experiment registration/report artifacts.
- Added tests for target/future leakage, ordering, numeric fitting, official season membership, and locked research readiness.
- Added a dashboard research view; all best-bet/shortlist qualification remains disabled. The final performance test remains unevaluated.
- Credentials, raw history, odds snapshots and fitted model artifacts stay outside version control. No paid services or scheduled jobs were enabled.
